Services

At Dreamweaver, I specialize in immersive forms of hypnosis: past-life regressions and guided inner journeys. We get started with a free, one-on-one consultation call, which you can Book Now to get started.

Great jumping off points for a first session together are thoughts like:

  • I feel stuck and I’d like to get unstuck
  • It’s time to move from one phase of my life into another
  • I’d like to find inspiration within myself for .
  • I’d like to feel differently (better!) about .
  • I’d like to stop doing .
  • I’d like to start doing .

From any of these, I’ll adapt my approach to best suit the outcome you’re looking for. I draw inspiration and techniques from Ericksonian hypnosis, conversational changework, neurolinguistic programming, and internal family systems. We’ll use the approach that works best for you.

Some people come to me because they’re just curious about finding out more about themselves, the guided inner journeys and past-life regressions I do, or hypnosis in general. That’s cool too!

I use the terms guided inner journeys and past-life regressions to refer to fully immersive hypnosis experiences where all of your senses are engaged in an internal experience. Simultaneously, you and I are still able to communicate about your experience as well as guide and shape that inner experience. It can feel similar to being in a dream while having someone “on the outside” who is able to help you navigate the dream experience.

This form of work is particularly good for gathering insight and inspiration and removing internal blockages.

I differ from many practitioners who offer past-life regressions in that I don’t have hard and fast beliefs about what is happening during the past-life regression experience. I even leave room for the possibility that the experiences in past-life regressions are not actually memories of past-lives at all. Whether this is true or not, I don’t think this detracts from their power or usefulness whatsoever.

My approach to my practice is based on compassion, play & humor, curiosity, and openness. The goal is to help you grow in a way that’s gentle while being precise and empowering to you.

The mission of Dreamweaver is to guide people to use their imaginations to create more possibilities and live a life they dream of living. The work is founded on the belief that we all have the resources we need to be healthy and happy within ourselves and we can use those resources to create a more beautiful world.

“That’s where I’d go, if I could go, that’s who I’d be, if I could be.” — Samuel Beckett

Sessions are typically in-person at my studio in Ridgewood, Queens. We can discuss if you believe remote work may be right for you, or if you are otherwise unable to attend.

Once we’ve had our initial consultation, we can book you in for a session. A standard session is 60 to 90 minutes in length and is priced at $200.
