About Dreamweaver

My name is Rob Hogan. Dreamweaver Hypnosis is my New York-based practice where I guide clients in using their imaginations to overcome limitations and live lives they dream of living.

I specialize in immersive forms of hypnosis: past-life regressions and guided inner journeys. I’m trained in standard forms of hypnosis and conversational formats that don’t require a traditional trance.

Whether you want to access inspiration, get unstuck, change an old habit, move past something that happened in the past, or merely discover more about yourself, we adapt the practice to your needs.
